What does Home Concepts do?

Home Concepts is a handloom and home-furnishing store in Malerkotla, Punjab. We design, stitch and install custom curtains, bed & home linen, and upholstery — from fabric selection to the final fitting.

Do you stitch curtains to fit my exact windows?

Yes. We measure your windows, help you choose fabric and lining, and stitch every curtain to size — including sheers, layered drapes and blackout options.

Can you reupholster a sofa or chair I already own?

Yes — we re-cover and rebuild sofas, chairs and banquette seating with new fabric, foam and finishing at our workshop in Malerkotla.

Do you handle installation, or do I arrange that myself?

Our own team fits everything on-site, from curtain tracks to finished upholstery, so there's nothing extra for you to arrange.

How do I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp with your room photos or measurements, call us directly, or visit our showroom on College Road — we're also happy to visit your home to measure and advise on fabric.

Have a space in mind?

Tell us what you're looking for — we'll help with fabric, finish and fit.
